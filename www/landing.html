Document Requests

Need school records? Here is how to request your documents.

Form 137 / SF10

Permanent Record. Request at the Registrar's Office. Processing time: 3-5 days.

Certificate of Enrollment

Proof of current enrollment. Available at the Guidance Office. Processing time: 1 day.

Diploma / Good Moral

For graduates and transferees. Request at the Principal's Office. Processing time: 2-3 days.
